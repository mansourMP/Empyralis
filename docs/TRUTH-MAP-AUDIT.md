## FACADES, ranked by how misleading

1. **Sage's self-described capabilities** — "full access to your computer," "I can control your computer," "search your machine," stated as present fact, unprompted, to every single brand-new account before they've connected anything. This is the very first thing a stranger sees this product claim about itself, and it's false in the moment it's said. (The refusal underneath is honest, which is exactly why this is fixable without touching enforcement — fix the self-description, not the gate.)
2. **Sage/operator cost is invisible everywhere, permanently, by construction** — not a display bug but a structurally missing write path. Directly contradicts the platform's own stated promise ("Honest costs... you always see exactly what you spend") for the one agent every user talks to before creating anything else.
3. **Tool toggle for core/safe-default tools does nothing** — live-proven for Web Search with a clean before/after trace. An owner who disables a tool believing it's now off has no way to discover it isn't, short of watching the turn trace.
4. **Workspace pulse strip rounds real spend to $0.00** — smaller in isolation, but compounds #2: two numbers on the same screen, both claiming to represent "today's cost," disagree, and the more visible one is the wrong one.
5. **The "Chat" tab's "customer would" framing tests the wrong authority tier** — lower severity (nothing is unsafe; the real gate is fine), but anyone using this surface to sanity-check mandate restrictions before shipping a channel will get a false "looks safe" signal.

Items 1–3 are the deploy blockers. A stranger's first five minutes includes items 1 and 2 by default (talk to Sage, ask what it can do, note the cost), and item 3 is discoverable by any owner who tries to actually rely on a tool toggle.
