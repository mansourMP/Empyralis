# Empyralis — Get Started

Welcome to Empyralis. Sage is your AI assistant — it reads your emails, manages your calendar, searches your Drive, remembers facts about you, and runs commands. All from chat or Telegram.

## 1. Open the app

Go to **[your Empyralis URL]** in your browser.

You'll see a page asking for your API key.

## 2. Paste your Anthropic API key

Get one at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys) (you'll need an account and prepaid credits — $5 is plenty to start).

Paste the key (starts with `sk-ant-`) and click **Continue**. Your key is stored encrypted and never leaves the server.

## 3. Connect Gmail (optional, one click)

Go to **Settings** → click **Connect** next to "Google Workspace". A new tab opens — sign in to your Google account and approve the requested permissions (read-only: Gmail, Calendar, Drive).

Once authorized, close that tab and return to Settings. You'll see "Connected."

## 4. Start chatting

Go to **Chat** and type a message. Try:

- "What are my latest 3 emails?"
- "What's on my calendar today?"
- "Remember that my dog's name is Max"

Sage responds as it types. It can use any connected app without asking for permission.

## 5. Telegram (optional)

To chat with Sage on Telegram instead of the browser:

1. Create a bot with [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) — you'll get a token
2. Give the token to whoever set up your Empyralis server
3. Once connected, message your bot on Telegram just like the web chat

The bot works 24/7 — no need to keep the browser open.
